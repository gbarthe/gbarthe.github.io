I am scientific director at the Max Planck Institute for Security and Privacy (MPI-SP) in Bochum, Germany, and a part-time research professor at IMDEA Software Institute in Madrid, Spain.

Research

My research interests include:

  • Computer security, cryptography, differential privacy
  • Programming languages and formal verification
  • Logic and foundations of computer science

Current projects

  • EasyCrypt: https://easycrypt.info/
    A toolset for reasoning about cryptographic constructions and proving their security.
  • Jasmin: https://jasmin-lang.org/
    A language and toolchain for high-assurance, high-performance cryptographic implementations.
  • FORMOSA: https://formosa-crypto.org/
    Formal methods for high-assurance cryptography.

Past projects (selection)

  • CertiCrypt: https://github.com/EasyCrypt/certicrypt
    Machine-checked cryptographic proofs.
  • CertiPriv: https://github.com/initc3/certipriv
    Machine-checked differential privacy proofs.
  • CT-verif: https://github.com/imdea-software/verifying-constant-time
    Automated verification of cryptographic constant-time.
  • maskverif: https://cryptoexperts.com/maskverif/
    Language-based, compositional, verification of higher-order masking.
